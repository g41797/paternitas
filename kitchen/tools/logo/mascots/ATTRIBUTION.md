## What was changed

The mascots themselves are not redrawn.

These files are new work, built from them:

- `paternitas-logo.svg`
    - Ziggy and Zero look at each other.
    - Each holds up a blue mask on a stick, in front of its eye.
    - A thin orange thread joins the two sticks.
- `paternitas-mask.svg`
    - Ziggy twice and Zero once, smaller, in a row.
    - Each one holds up the same blue mask on a stick.
    - One thin thread joins the sticks.
    - Text labels are added.
- `favicon.svg` and `favicon.ico`
    - Only the mask and its stick, seen from the front, on orange.
    - No mascot is in them.

`gen_logo.py` in this folder draws all of them from the two copies here.
